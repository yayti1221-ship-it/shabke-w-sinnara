OUR STORY

Roots in Sidon's ancient fishing culture.

For generations, our family has lived in harmony with the Mediterranean. Under the soft amber glow of Lebanese dawns, we sailed the historic waters of Sidon, returning with treasures of the deep. Shabke w Sinnara brings this timeless romance straight to your culinary plate.

Every catch is hand-selected at the local port by our skilled captains. Cooked with high culinary craft, our dishes are an exceptional fusion of time-honored Lebanese traditions and state-of-the-art modern gourmet styling.
